How it works

From check-up numbers to one clear estimate.

STEP 01

Enter your measurements

Age, blood pressure, cholesterol, glucose, smoking and a few yes/no history questions. Anything you don't know can stay blank.

STEP 02

Compare with real patient patterns

The model was trained on thousands of Framingham participants who were followed for ten years.

STEP 03

Get a risk percentage

Scores above the model's alert threshold are flagged as higher risk. The threshold was chosen to balance missed cases against false alarms.

STEP 04

Take it to a professional

This is a screening aid for learning, not a diagnosis. A high score is a reason to talk to a doctor, not to worry.

A screening aid, not a verdict

Most people in this dataset never developed heart disease, so a model that always says “healthy” would score a high accuracy and still be useless. That's why Cardia reports ranking quality and recall instead. Predicting ten years ahead from routine measurements is genuinely hard: even established clinical risk scores land near 0.75 AUC. Treat every result as a conversation starter for a doctor's visit.
